CEO Succession Planning

A disciplined, transparent, and data-driven succession process — from profile development to board advisory to transition planning. Designed for boards that take continuity seriously.

1

CEO Success Profile Development

Integrating external market benchmarks with company-specific strategic priorities, leadership expectations, and cultural requirements to define exactly what the next CEO must embody.

2

Candidate Assessment

Structured competency-based executive interviews, psychometrics assessment suite, 360-degree leadership feedback, and evaluation against the bespoke success profile.

3

Targeted Development Interventions

Executive coaching for internal candidates over 3–6 months, or strategic leadership interventions to close identified capability gaps against the success profile.

4

Board Reporting & Advisory

Written assessment summaries, comparative insights, practical recommendations, and facilitated Board discussions with the HR/Nomination Committee.

5

Transition Planning

Structured CEO transition and onboarding plan ensuring continuity, stakeholder confidence, and momentum for the incoming leader from day one.
